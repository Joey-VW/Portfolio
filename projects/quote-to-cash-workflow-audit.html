Transparent method

Generated on purpose, audited like production data

Deterministic source

A fixed seed creates separate CRM, billing, and revenue entities with varied plans, segments, amounts, timings, statuses, and injected exceptions.

Independent cohorts

Close, activation, and recognition intervals each use the records eligible for that one calculation. No blanket dropna().

Integrity first

Duplicate keys, broken foreign keys, ineligible subscriptions, missing stages, impossible dates, stalls, and long delays are measured.

Production next steps

With governed source systems, I would add owner queues, trend monitoring, segment thresholds, lineage, alerts, and forecast reconciliation.
