EV COST CHECK

The Real Cost of Public Charging

Don't just ditch the gas, ditch the station too.

We moved from a Honda Pilot to a Kia EV9, but we do not have home charging yet. One public charging receipt made the real question clear: where does an EV become cheaper?

Our starting point

We bought our 2026 Kia EV9 at a very generous price, from a very close friend, replacing our 2018 Honda Pilot.

I was still curious about the fuel savings. With no home charger yet, I compared the Pilot’s gasoline cost with the EV9’s first public fast-charging receipt. The result genuinely surprised me: the EV was costing more per mile.

Apparently I had missed the most important memo. The biggest savings do not come from simply leaving gasoline behind; they come from leaving the station behind, too. Public charging is convenient. Home charging is what changes the math.

I put this model together to show where the answer flips, and to give other families a practical idea of what to expect. It compares energy cost per mile, not every cost of owning a vehicle, and it will get sharper as our real driving and charging history grows.
