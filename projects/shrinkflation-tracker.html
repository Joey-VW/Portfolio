Shrinkflation is when a product gets smaller while its shelf price stays the same or rises. The price tag may look familiar, but the cost per unit goes up.

How to read the tracker

  1. Find the signal. Cards tagged “Shrinkflation” pair a smaller package with a higher unit cost.

  2. Compare all three numbers. Read package size, shelf price, and price per unit together to see what really changed.

  3. Follow and filter. Mini charts show the direction over time; filters and the detail table help you dig deeper.
