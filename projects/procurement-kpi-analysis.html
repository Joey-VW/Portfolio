Case study

Procurement KPI Analysis

Supplier KPI system for cost, savings, lead time, delivery reliability, defects, and compliance.

Back to projectsDiscuss this work

Problem

Procurement teams often inherit disconnected spreadsheets and vendor extracts that make it difficult to see supplier health quickly.

Solution

Modeled procurement events into reusable KPI views, added validation checks, and designed a dashboard narrative around supplier cost, reliability, and quality.

Tech stack

SQLBigQueryPythonpandasLooker StudioData QA

Key features

  • Supplier scorecard cards
  • Negotiation savings logic
  • Lead-time and late-delivery signals
  • Defect-rate and compliance views

Architecture / data flow

Source exports land in staging tables, Python profiles the data, SQL creates clean facts and dimensions, and dashboard views surface supplier trends.

Impact

Provides a clear executive view of which suppliers create cost, delivery, or quality risk.

What I’d improve next

Add scheduled ingestion, stronger anomaly detection, and documented metric contracts.
