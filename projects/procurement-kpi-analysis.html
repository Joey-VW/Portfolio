Reproducible preparation

One source, one metric layer, two delivery paths

The public case study stays static and credential-free. BigQuery remains an optional documented warehouse target.

  1. 01
    Preserve the source

    The retrieved CSV stays unchanged and is identified by SHA-256.

  2. 02
    Enforce the contract

    Identifiers, domains, quantities, prices, defects, dates, and exception counts are checked.

  3. 03
    Build the metric layer

    Supplier, category, and monthly outputs use the same documented formulas.

  4. 04
    Publish static JSON

    The browser reads a deterministic artifact; optional BigQuery loading is separate.

Method + limits

What the score can and cannot say

Metric rules

  • On time means delivery within 10 calendar days.
  • Missing delivery dates leave delivery denominators.
  • Missing defect counts are unknown, not zero.
  • Savings are quantity-weighted.

Quality treatment

  • Blocking contract failures stop the build.
  • Expected audit exceptions remain measured.
  • Impossible deliveries are quarantined from delivery metrics.
  • Every browser value comes from maintained code.

Limitations

  • The dataset publisher describes the records as anonymized.
  • Supplier labels and outcomes are not independently verified.
  • The 10-day target is an analytical assumption.
  • Scores are sensitive to the chosen weights and peer set.
